---

# Why Are We Learning This?

Let's think about Magento.

When a customer clicks **Add to Cart**, Magento doesn't simply add the product.

It first asks questions like:

```text
Is the product enabled?

↓

Is the product in stock?

↓

Does the customer have permission?

↓

Is quantity available?
```

Every answer is either:

```text
Yes

or

No
```

Computers don't "think."

They evaluate conditions.

This is exactly what operators and conditions allow us to do.
